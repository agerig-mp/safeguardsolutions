SAFETY

EVERY PERSON WHO SHOWS UP GOES HOME.

That is the standard we build every jobsite around. Not a policy in a binder. The reason our crews work the way they do.

WHY IT MATTERS

TWO FAMILIES ARE COUNTING ON EVERY DECISION WE MAKE.

Ours and yours. Our crew goes home to theirs at the end of the day. Your project goes back to an owner who needs it delivered on schedule and without an incident anyone has to explain. Safety is the one thing that protects both at the same time, so we treat it like the job itself, not the paperwork around it.

HOW WE WORK

THE STANDARD SHOWS UP BEFORE THE CREW DOES.

Three commitments. Every crew, every site, every day. This is what a Safeguard job looks like before anyone touches a tool.

01

PLAN THE WORK

Every job starts with a Job Hazard Analysis before a single post goes in the ground. Site conditions, utilities, access, and hazards get walked, documented, and reviewed with the crew that will do the work.

02

ANYONE CAN STOP IT

Every person on our crew has the authority to stop work. No permission needed, no questions asked, no consequence for using it.

03

TRAINED BEFORE TASKED

Nobody gets put on a task they have not been trained for. Certifications stay current and the office tracks them, not the field.

WHO OWNS IT

EVERY PERSON. ALL THE WAY UP.

Every person on a Safeguard site is responsible for a safe project. That is not a slogan we hand to the field and walk away from. Accountability runs up the chain, through the foreman, through operations, to the President of this company. If something goes wrong on one of our jobs, it lands on us, not on the crew.

Monday Morning Toolbox Talk

Every week starts together, on topic, before anyone hits a site.
