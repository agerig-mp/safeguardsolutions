LOCAL / CONNERSVILLE, INDIANA

THE RIVER TOOK DECADES IN ONE NIGHT.

In August 2026, the Whitewater River crested at nearly 22 feet and tore through Fayette County. At Robinson’s Whitewater River Campground, floodwater picked up more than a hundred campers and threw them into the woods. Dwain Robinson had spent decades building that place. It was gone in a matter of hours.

The United Cajun Navy came up from the Gulf to help.

AES Indiana asked us to come stand beside them.

On Friday, August 21st, we shut the company down. No jobs. No schedule. No exceptions. Eighteen people, thirty-six hands, six trucks, four pieces of equipment, and three trailers drove to Connersville and spent the day working alongside the United Cajun Navy in what was left of the campground.

We brought the hands and the skid steers. We cleared debris, sorted what could be saved from what could not, and moved out the trailers insurance had already written off. We dug four wheelers out from where the current had wedged them. We carried heavy things out of spaces families had spent years making their own, next to the people who had built them.

NATIONAL / WESTERN NORTH CAROLINA

SOME OF IT FINISHED THE TRIP BY MULE.

When Hurricane Helene tore through Western North Carolina and Eastern Tennessee in the fall of 2024, it took the roads with it. Communities that needed help most were the ones nobody could drive to.

AES Indiana asked us to help. We were barely a year into this business. We said yes anyway, and we brought Citizens State Bank of New Castle with us.

Our team spent a day buying thousands of dollars in supplies. Truckloads of it went south to the Carolina Cavalry warehouse in Asheville. The rest went up into the mountains the only way left, hauled the final miles by side by side, by mule team, and by helicopter.

“When that call came we were barely a year in. There was no version of this where we said no. That is not something we marketed. It is something that got built into this company while we were still figuring out who we were. We have a group of men and women here who will do whatever they can when somebody asks, and this company was a family before it was anything else.”

Ted Brown V

President
