CHALLENGES & SOLUTIONS

PROJECT CHALLENGES AND OUR RESPONSE

How the project team addressed high-risk utility perimeter conditions with practical, layered controls.

PROJECT CHALLENGES

  • Long perimeter stretches with limited passive deterrence
  • Multiple vehicle access points increasing exposure
  • Need to execute upgrades without utility service disruption
  • Existing perimeter elements nearing end of lifecycle

OUR SOLUTIONS

  • Installed anti-climb perimeter fence upgrades at priority segments
  • Reconfigured controlled access corridors for authorized traffic flow
  • Integrated barrier and bollard layers near high-value zones
  • Phased implementation plan to support continuous operations
